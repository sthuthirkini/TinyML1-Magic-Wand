## Magic Wand — Baseline Model

**Purpose:** Recognize Circle, W, and Z gestures using an ESP32 and accelerometer.

### Dataset
- Training: 34 Circle, 30 W, 30 Z
- Testing: 30 samples total
- Note: Test samples were modified during debugging, so results are provisional.

### Impulse
- Window size: 3 seconds
- DSP blocks: Spectral Analysis + Flatten
- Classifier: Fully connected neural network
- Hidden layers: 20 neurons, then 10 neurons
- Output classes: Circle, W, Z
- Training cycles: 100
- Learning rate: 0.0005
- Validation split: 20%
- Batch size: 32
- Int8 profiling: Enabled

### Baseline results
- Validation accuracy: approximately 94.7%
- Validation loss: approximately 0.27
- Test accuracy: 60%
- F1 scores: Circle 0.56, W 0.75, Z 0.78
- Estimated model size: approximately 15.5 KB
- Estimated inference time: approximately 1 ms

### Known issues
- Circle and W are sometimes confused.
- Some predictions are uncertain.
- Test results are not an independent final benchmark.
